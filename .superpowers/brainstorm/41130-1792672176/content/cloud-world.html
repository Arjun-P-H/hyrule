The cloud world under the islands: which shape feels right?

Side views, not to scale. The hero jumps about 1.2 m and glides about 3 m forward for every 1 m it drops, so a cloud you climb up to has to be a short hop away, while one below can be far off.

A. Rolling dunes

A soft, walkable floor of low swells, with a few puffs drifting above. It's calm and easy to cross. The islands stay far overhead, so you need the puffs or a portal to reach them.

B. Towering stacks

Big cumulus towers rise out of deep valleys, and some reach nearly up to the islands. Staircases of puffs lead up their sides. The highs and lows are dramatic, and the towers are the way up.

C. Layered shelves

A hilly cloud floor with broad shelves floating at a few heights, joined by small puffs. It reads as levels you climb tier by tier toward the islands, which makes it the most platformer-like.
